# Flipkart Sales Analysis

## Overview

This project analyzes Flipkart sales data using SQL and Power BI. The objective is to understand sales performance, profitability, product performance, customer segments, discounts, and regional trends, and to identify key business insights from the data.

## Dataset

The dataset contains Flipkart sales transaction data with the following columns:

- **Order ID:** Unique identifier for each order.
- **Product Name:** Name of the product.
- **Category:** Product category.
- **Price (INR):** Price of the product.
- **Quantity Sold:** Number of units sold.
- **Total Sales (INR):** Total sales generated.
- **Order Date:** Date of the order.
- **Payment Method:** Payment method used for the order.
- **Customer Rating:** Rating given by the customer.
- **Month:** Month of the order.
- **Year:** Year of the order.
- **Profit (INR):** Profit generated from the order.
- **Discount:** Discount offered on the product.
- **Customer Segment:** Customer segment.
- **Region:** Region where the order was placed.

## Tools Used

- **SQL (PostgreSQL):** Data analysis and business queries.
- **Power BI:** Data visualization and dashboard development.
- **Power Query:** Data cleaning and transformation.
- **DAX:** Measures and KPI calculations.

## Analysis

The analysis includes the following areas:

1. **Sales Analysis**
   - Total Sales
   - Total Orders
   - Average Order Value (AOV)
   - Sales by Category
   - Sales by Region
   - Monthly Sales Trends

2. **Profitability Analysis**
   - Total Profit
   - Profit Margin
   - Profit by Category
   - Profit by Region
   - Profit by Customer Segment

3. **Product Analysis**
   - Top 10 Products by Sales
   - Product-wise Profit
   - Product Sales Performance

4. **Customer Segment Analysis**
   - Sales by Customer Segment
   - Profit by Customer Segment
   - Orders by Customer Segment
   - Average Order Value by Segment

5. **Discount Analysis**
   - Discount Distribution
   - Sales by Discount Band
   - Profitability across different discount levels

6. **Rating Analysis**
   - Customer Rating Distribution
   - Rating vs Sales
   - Rating vs Profit

## Dashboard

The Power BI dashboard provides an interactive view of:

- Sales and Profit KPIs
- Sales by Category
- Sales by Region
- Top Products
- Customer Segments
- Discount Analysis
- Customer Ratings
- Monthly Sales Trends

## Key Insights

- Electronics generated the highest sales among the analyzed categories.
- Sales and profitability were analyzed together to identify categories that generate both revenue and profit.
- Discount levels were compared with sales and profitability to understand their impact on business performance.
- Regional and customer-segment analysis was used to identify differences in sales performance.
- Product-level analysis helped identify the products contributing the most to overall sales.

Conclusion

This project demonstrates how SQL and Power BI can be used together to analyze e-commerce sales data. The analysis focuses on understanding sales performance, profitability, customer segments, products, discounts, and regional trends to support data-driven business decisions.
